Lanternlight — bring light to the night

How to play

Bring light to the night meadow. Set your six lanterns in turn, each lit by up to four of your twelve fireflies — every lantern must hold at least one, so leave enough for those still to come. Every patch of ground glows for whichever light falls on it brightest. Light fades fast as it travels: four fireflies shine only twice as far as one — but where glows meet, the brighter simply wins; press four fireflies against a lone one and the dim lantern keeps only a small pocket around itself. Spread thin to touch more of the night; burn bright to take and hold it. Ground no light reaches stays dark and counts for no one.
